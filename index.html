All 145 topics in one list, each holding the recommendations made about it. Contested decisions come first: points where included studies point in different directions, which the literature does not settle for you. Below them, every design issue, most-studied first. Bar length shows how many independent studies take each position.

Counts are independent studies, not reports: two pairs of papers report the same study and are counted once. A count shows how often a position recurs in this literature, not how strong the evidence for it is. “Direct” means studies that generated evidence of their own, as opposed to reviews and conceptual papers.

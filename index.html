Twelve design areas. A recommendation sits in the area it is mostly about, though the areas overlap: visual design needs simplicity, sensory control needs visual design. Pick an area to filter, or search.

The 23 disagreements come first: points where included studies point in different directions, which the literature does not settle for you. Below them, every recommendation, most-studied first.

Counts are independent studies, not reports: three pairs of papers report the same study each and are counted once. A count shows how often a position recurs in this literature, not how strong the evidence for it is. “Evidence of their own” means studies that generated data, as opposed to reviews and conceptual papers. 193 of the 420 recommendations here, 46 per cent, come from a single study.
